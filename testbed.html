List projects

Glycolytic Regulation of Retinal Health and Degeneration Dr. Camacho's Project

Dr. Camacho’s research investigates how key glycolytic enzymes, particularly triosephosphate isomerase (TPI) and phosphofructokinase (PFK), regulate retinal metabolism and influence susceptibility to retinal degeneration. Her lab examines how disruptions in energy production affect photoreceptor survival, retinal function, and responses to environmental stressors.

resource
Retinal Metabolomics and Cellular Responses to Degeneration Victoria Lemus' Project

Victoria’s research examines metabolic changes that occur during retinal degeneration and recovery. Using metabolomic profiling and molecular analyses, she investigates how retinal cells alter energy utilization and biochemical pathways following injury or disease. Her work aims to identify metabolic signatures associated with retinal stress and uncover potential therapeutic targets for preserving vision.

metabolomics degeneration retinitis-pigmentosa
Blue Light-Induced Retinal Degeneration Addison Kanke's Project

Addison’s research investigates how blue light exposure affects retinal health, with a particular focus on intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells (ipRGCs). These specialized retinal neurons are responsible for non-image-forming visual functions, including circadian rhythm regulation, pupillary light reflexes, and light-mediated behavioral responses. Her work examines the vulnerability and adaptive responses of ipRGCs to blue light-induced stress and explores how alterations in these cells contribute to retinal dysfunction and neurodegeneration.
